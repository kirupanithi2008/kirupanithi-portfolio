Cyber-AI

Privacy-first cybersecurity assistant
MAIN PROJECT

Cyber-AI is a protective cybersecurity platform designed to help users understand common digital threats. It aims to analyze suspicious links, messages and phone-related risks, then present clear findings, privacy guidance and a consolidated security report.

Link ShieldChecks suspicious URL patterns and explains possible risks.
Message ShieldHelps identify scam, phishing and social-engineering signals.
Phone ShieldProvides awareness about phone-number-related risks without exposing private data.
Identity GuardOrganizes identity, privacy and account-safety concerns.
